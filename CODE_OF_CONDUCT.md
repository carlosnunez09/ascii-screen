# Code of Conduct

Be kind and be useful.

- Treat everyone with respect. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Keep feedback specific and constructive, and assume good intent.
- Do not post other people's private information.
- AI-assisted contributions are welcome; low-effort spam and unchecked bulk output are not.

The maintainer may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may block people who repeatedly do. To report a problem, contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile.

This applies to all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews.
